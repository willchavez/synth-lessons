Filter

Cutoff sets the starting point. Envelope amount sets how far it moves.

Filter envelope

How quickly the cutoff moves out, settles, holds, and returns. Changes apply while you hold a note.

Amp envelope

How quickly volume rises, settles, holds, and fades out. Changes apply while you hold a note.

LFO

Rate = how fast. Amount = how far. Zero amount turns the movement off.
