one note,
three moving parts.

The filter decides which frequencies get through. The filter envelope moves the cutoff over the life of the note. The amp envelope separately shapes how loud the note is.

oscillator → filter + filter envelope → amplifier + amp envelope → output
cutoff28%
The filter's resting position. Lower = darker. Higher = brighter.
resonance18%
Emphasizes frequencies right around the cutoff. More = peakier / squelchier.

How far the filter envelope is allowed to move the cutoff. It is a depth control.
